A multiplayer poker room-service codebase

The repository presents core C++ / Tars services behind online Texas Hold'em rooms and tables. Room, player, table, message and order interfaces cover matching, seating, betting, spectating, disconnect recovery and result collection. It is positioned for technical evaluation and lawful secondary development, not as an unsupported one-click production claim.

Inside the product

Buy-in workflow

From seat selection to chips at the table

The buy-in interface lets a player choose an allowed amount before active play. Server handlers coordinate balance, seating and room rules, while private-room settings can control the rebuy multiple.

  • Room-specific buy-in limits
  • Player seating and game-state coordination
  • Owner-defined rebuy rules

Club operations

Traceable fund issuance and club records

The club screens show account changes and transaction history. They complement the room-service club data and give operators a readable workflow.

  • Fund issuance and confirmation
  • Member balance history
  • Club room and management information

Operations workflow

Fund transfers linked to club administration

The transfer screen illustrates the operational layer around private and club rooms. Production use must connect these actions to authentication, persistent records, risk controls and lawful payment procedures.

  • Recipient and amount workflow
  • Confirmation before record creation
  • Production controls remain an integration responsibility
